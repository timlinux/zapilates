About

Marcelle Volckaert

A South African soprano captivating audiences across continents with her lyrical artistry and profound musicality.

Based in the Western Cape, Marcelle Volckaert brings a wealth of experience from performances across South Africa, Europe, and beyond. Her repertoire spans from baroque to contemporary, with particular expertise in art song, chamber music, and opera scenes.
