## Group Pilates Classes

Group classes offer the benefits of Pilates in a social setting. Classes are kept small to ensure individual attention while enjoying the motivation and energy of working with others.

Classes include Matwork and Pilates Equipment sessions suitable for various levels.

Contact Marcelle to join a group class or enquire about the current timetable.
