## Booking notes

- **Remedial classes** — please have your doctor's clearance to hand and be ready to describe your issue so we can discuss suitability before the first session.
- **Musicians & performers** — let me know your instrument and the specific tension patterns you're working with.
- **Online classes** — you'll need a device with a camera, a stable internet connection, and a small clear space (a chair is enough for Barre).
- **Payment** in Euros; international card payments accepted.
