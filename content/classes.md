Choose the format that fits — from **one-to-one attention** to a **spirited barre class**, from **beginners** to **remedial rehabilitation**. All classes teach the same principles: *breath, centre, alignment, control, flow*.

- **[Private](/private/)** — one-to-one, tailored to your body's needs.
- **[Group](/group/)** — small classes, matwork and equipment.
- **[Barre](/barre/)** — online, ballet-inspired, chair-based.
- **[Remedial](/remedial/)** — small groups or private, for diagnosed back / neck / hip issues.

Not sure which is right? [Get in touch](/contact/) — a short conversation is usually enough to point you at the right starting place.
