## Pilates Barre Class

### Goals include:

- Joy in movement through dance inspiration and flow coupled with Pilates benefits
- Freedom of movement, strength and flexibility
- Learn how to address and correct the muscular imbalances in the body
- Improved fitness
- Improved coordination and alignment
- Physical and psychological benefits
- A respite from your social isolation!

### What:

- Online instructor-led Pilates based, ballet inspired barre and chair classes
- Suitable for healthy bodies or those wishing to improve their fitness - not remedial
- Only a chair, little space and a device with a camera and an internet connection required
- A fun, inspiring class which combines beautiful music, ballet and Pilates principles with a highly qualified and experienced teacher
