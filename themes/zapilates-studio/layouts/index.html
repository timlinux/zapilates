About Marcelle

A body that holds together.

I have been teaching Pilates for over twenty years, on three continents and in different languages.

From my introduction to ballet at four years old, through my study of the human body under Jennifer Paynter and full instructor certification with STOTT Pilates (Canada) and PTAF (South Africa), my search has always been the same: how to make the body work correctly.

I teach matwork, equipment and barre — as well as remedial classes and workshops for musicians, dancers, performing artists and other specialists.
