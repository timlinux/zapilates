{{< lead >}}
Improve pain, misalignment and physical well-being by applying the Pilates principles to a set of exercises suitable for home practice.
{{< /lead >}}

Remedial lessons focus intensively on individual corrections, in small groups of only three, so a diagnosed issue gets the attention it needs.

{{< checklist title="Goals" >}}

- Freedom of movement, strength and flexibility, without pain
- Correction of muscular imbalances
- A better understanding of your body and how it functions
- Improved coordination and alignment
{{< /checklist >}}

{{< checklist title="What it is" >}}

- Small-group lessons — only three people per class, over a ten-lesson course
- Suitable for diagnosed back, neck and hip problems
- Intensive focus on individual corrections
- A high success rate with a highly qualified, experienced teacher
{{< /checklist >}}

{{< factsheet title="At a glance" >}}

- **Format:** Small group (max three), or private
- **Course:** Ten lessons
- **Cost:** €200 for the course (€20 per lesson)
- **Languages:** English, with some Portuguese and Afrikaans where needed
{{< /factsheet >}}

This course is also available as private tuition (different rates apply). Please discuss your suitability and goals first.
