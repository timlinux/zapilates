## Movement specialist

I have been involved in body work ever since my introduction to ballet at the age of four, studying classical ballet throughout my school years. It was through my ballet teacher — the highly esteemed Jennifer Paynter (RAD examiner and teacher trainer) — that I was introduced to the Pilates method as an adult.

An interest in body mechanics, anatomy and the search for *how to make the body work correctly* sparked an ongoing study of the human body: training in the Body Control Pilates method, followed by full instructor certification through Pilates Conditioning (PTAF, South Africa) and STOTT Pilates (Canada). I also hold a **Master's Degree in Music (cum laude, Stellenbosch University)**, in which I researched the benefits of the Pilates method for classical singers.

I teach matwork, equipment and barre, as well as remedial classes and workshops for musicians, dancers and performing artists. My passion for uncovering each student's potential is what makes teaching rewarding — and drives my continuous search for new ways of doing and understanding.

Along the way I have studied other somatic modalities — Yoga, Reiki, the Franklin Method, fascial integration work, Pranayama and meditation — combining what I have learnt into classes with a different focus for each student.
